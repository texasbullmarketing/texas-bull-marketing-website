FAQ

Questions that usually come up

What’s the difference between buying the site and GBP Management & Web Essentials? ▾

Standard is $700. Premium is $900 with the Premium add-on: English and Spanish, and a language switch. Both include domain, 1 year hosting, and 4 Lite Updates the first year — you own the site. After year 1, hosting is $20 a month or $200 a year, or $29 a month for hosting plus unlimited Lite Updates. GBP Management & Web Essentials is $250 a month for the website, Google Business Profile management, and unlimited Lite Updates.

Is the monthly plan required? ▾

No. You can buy the site, or use GBP Management & Web Essentials at $250 a month. Pick one path.

Do I own the site on the monthly plan? ▾

Buying Standard or Premium means you own the site. On GBP Management & Web Essentials you can buy it out at full price before 6 months ($700 Standard or $900 Premium) or 50% off after 6 months ($350 or $450). You can stop that monthly anytime.

Can I cancel monthly care? ▾

Yes. You can stop GBP Management & Web Essentials anytime. If you cancel the monthly without buying the site, the domain transfer is $50. A site you already bought is still yours.

What is a Lite Update? ▾

Small site edits: price changes, new photos, announcement banners. Buy the site and you get 4 the first year. GBP Management & Web Essentials includes unlimited Lite Updates. After year 1, site buyers can add unlimited Lite Updates with hosting for $29 a month.

Where is the site hosted? ▾

On a global edge network we use so sites stay fast and stay up, including in rural Texas with weak cell. Buy the site and the first year of hosting is included. After year 1, hosting is $20 a month or $200 a year, or $29 a month for hosting plus unlimited Lite Updates. GBP Management & Web Essentials includes hosting in the monthly.

Is the domain included? ▾

Yes. Buy the site and the first year of domain and hosting is included. GBP Management & Web Essentials includes domain and hosting while the monthly is active. If you cancel the monthly without buying the site, the domain transfer is $50.

Standard or Premium? ▾

Standard is $700. Premium is $900 with the Premium add-on: a second-language edition and a language switch. We recommend on the demo call.

What’s included in the free demo? ▾

A personalized mockup for your business so you can see layout, mobile, and CTAs with your name on it — then a short call to review. No obligation to buy.

How fast can we go live? ▾

Normally 3 to 5 days. We can go live as soon as you are happy with the site and pick a domain.

What do I need to provide? ▾

Business name, phone, address or service area, hours, services or menu, logo if you have one, and any photos. We guide you on the call.
