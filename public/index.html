FAQ

Questions that usually come up

What’s the difference between buying the site and Website + Google? ▾

Buy the site for $700. Premium is +$200, so the total is $900: second-language pages with their own URLs, such as /es/, so locals searching in Spanish can find the business, plus room for schema and extra pages. Same phone, hours, and map. Both include domain, 1 year hosting, and 4 Lite Updates the first year — you own the site. After year 1, hosting is $20 a month or $200 a year, or hosting and unlimited Lite Updates for $29 a month or $290 a year. GBP Management & Web Essentials is $250 a month for the website, Google Business Profile management, and unlimited Lite Updates.

Is the monthly plan required? ▾

No. You can buy the site, or use GBP Management & Web Essentials at $250 a month. Pick one path.

Do I own the site on the monthly plan? ▾

Buying the site means you own it. Standard is $700. Premium is $900. GBP Management & Web Essentials is the monthly for the website and Google. You can stop that monthly anytime.

Can I cancel the monthly plan? ▾

Yes. You can stop GBP Management & Web Essentials anytime. If you cancel the monthly without buying the site, the domain transfer is $50. A site you already bought is still yours.

What is a Lite Update? ▾

Small site edits: price changes, new photos, announcement banners. Buy the site and you get 4 the first year. GBP Management & Web Essentials includes unlimited Lite Updates. After year 1, site buyers can add hosting and unlimited Lite Updates for $29 a month or $290 a year.

Where is the site hosted? ▾

Built light, so it loads fast even when your phone only has one bar. Buy the site and the first year of hosting is included. After year 1, hosting is $20 a month or $200 a year, or hosting and unlimited Lite Updates for $29 a month or $290 a year. GBP Management & Web Essentials includes hosting in the monthly.

Is the domain included? ▾

Yes. Buy the site and the first year of domain and hosting is included. GBP Management & Web Essentials includes domain and hosting while the monthly is active. If you cancel the monthly without buying the site, the domain transfer is $50.

Standard or Premium? ▾

Standard is $700. Premium is +$200, so the total is $900. Premium adds second-language pages with their own URLs, such as /es/, so locals searching in Spanish can find the business, plus room for schema and extra pages. Same phone, hours, and map. We recommend on the demo call.

What’s included in the free demo? ▾

A personalized mockup for your business so you can see layout, mobile, and CTAs with your name on it — then a short call to review. No obligation to buy.

How fast can we go live? ▾

Normally 3 to 5 days. We can go live as soon as you are happy with the site and pick a domain.

What do I need to provide? ▾

Business name, phone, address or service area, hours, services or menu, logo if you have one, and any photos. We guide you on the call.
