FAQ

Questions owners ask.

What do I get if I buy the site?

With Premium off, the buy card is Standard at $700 one time. You get the website, the domain, 1 year of hosting, and 4 Lite Updates. You own the site. Google Business Profile management is not included.

What is the Premium add-on?

Premium is the add-on on that same buy card. It is +$200, so the total is $900 one time. You get everything in Standard, plus a second-language edition of your site and a language switch. You own the site.

What is GBP Management & Web Essentials?

It is $250 a month. You get the website and Google Business Profile management, unlimited Lite Updates, and the domain and hosting in the monthly. You can cancel anytime.

What does hosting cost after the first year if I buy the site?

If you bought the site, hosting after year 1 is $20 a month or $200 a year. Or $29 a month for hosting and unlimited Lite Updates. Monthly plans already include hosting, so this is only for buyers.

Can I buy the site later if I start monthly?

Before 6 months, the buyout is full price: $700 for Standard or $900 for Premium. After 6 months, the buyout is half: $350 for Standard or $450 for Premium.

Can I cancel a monthly plan?

Yes. You can cancel a monthly plan anytime.

What if I cancel and want the domain?

If you cancel a monthly plan and do not buy the site, the domain transfer is $50.

What is a Lite Update?

A small edit: a price, a photo, your hours, or a banner. If you buy the site, you get 4 Lite Updates in the first year. On GBP Management & Web Essentials, Lite Updates are unlimited.

Will the site load out in the country?

We build it light so it loads fast even with 1 bar in rural Texas.

A demo of your business with your name on it, so you can see the page on your phone before you buy. No obligation.
