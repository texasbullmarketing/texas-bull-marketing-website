FAQ

Questions that usually come up

What’s the difference between buying the site and Site + GBP? ▾

Standard is $700. Premium is $900 with the Premium add-on. Both include domain, 1 year hosting, and 4 Lite Updates — you own the site. Site + GBP is the other path: $150 setup, then $150/mo Standard or $250/mo Premium, with the live site and Google in the monthly. Google is not an add-on if you buy the site.

Is the monthly plan required? ▾

No. Buying the site or Site + GBP are two separate plans. Pick one path.

Is the $150 setup my first month too? ▾

No. $150 to start. The $150 or $250 monthly begins when you are happy with the site and ready to go live.

Do I own the site on the monthly plan? ▾

Monthly is a lease with an option to buy. After 6 months you can buy the site at 50% off — $350 Standard or $450 Premium. Before 6 months, the buyout is full price.

Can I cancel monthly care? ▾

Yes. You can stop anytime. If you want to keep the site, the buyout is full price before 6 months, and 50% off after 6 months.

What is a Lite Update? ▾

Small site edits: price changes, new photos, announcement banners. Buy the site and you get 4 per year. Site + GBP includes 1 per month.

Where is the site hosted? ▾

On a global edge network we use so sites stay fast and stay up, including in rural Texas with weak cell. Buy the site and the first year of hosting is included. Site + GBP includes hosting in the monthly.

Is the domain included? ▾

Yes. Buy the site and the first year of domain and hosting is included. Site + GBP includes domain and hosting in the monthly.

Standard or Premium? ▾

Standard is $700, or $150 setup + $150/mo. Premium is the add-on — $900, or $150 setup + $250/mo — with a second-language edition and a language switch. We recommend on the demo call.

What’s included in the free demo? ▾

A personalized mockup for your business so you can see layout, mobile, and CTAs with your name on it — then a short call to review. No obligation to buy.

How fast can we go live? ▾

Normally 3 to 5 days. We can go live as soon as you are happy with the site and pick a domain.

What do I need to provide? ▾

Business name, phone, address or service area, hours, services or menu, logo if you have one, and any photos. We guide you on the call.
