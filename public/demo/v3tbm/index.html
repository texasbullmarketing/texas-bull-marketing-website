FAQ

Questions that usually come up

Is the monthly plan required? ▾

No. You can buy the website setup on its own. Most owners add Google Business Profile management because Maps is where local customers look first.

Can I cancel monthly care? ▾

Yes. No long lock-in games on monthly GBP / light site care. You keep the site you paid to build; hosting after year one is discussed up front so you’re not surprised.

What’s included in the free demo? ▾

A personalized mockup for your business so you can see layout, mobile, and CTAs with your name on it — then a short call to review. No obligation to buy.

English only or bilingual? ▾

Both options exist. We recommend based on your customers and market on the demo call. Bilingual means real Spanish pages and a language switch. Spanish is an add-on to the site setup.

How fast can we go live? ▾

After you approve scope and send logo, photos, hours, and services, many straightforward sites launch in about 5 business days. Complex menus or missing assets take longer.

What do I need to provide? ▾

Business name, phone, address or service area, hours, services or menu, logo if you have one, and any photos. We guide you on the call.
